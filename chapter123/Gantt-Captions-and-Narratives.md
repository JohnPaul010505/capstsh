## Figure 29

**Caption:** Figure 29: Gantt Chart (Work Plan)

**Narrative:**

> Figure 29 presents the Gantt chart of the development of the proposed
> Intelligent Fitness Progress Monitoring Application of Triple J Fitness Center.
> The chart covers the months of January to September and follows the four phases
> of the Rapid Application Development (RAD) methodology discussed in Section 3.1.
> The proponents began with Planning in January to February, followed by Data Gathering
> through observation and face-to-face interviews in February to March. Group
> meetings were conducted regularly from January to August to keep the development
> of the system coordinated. The appropriate software to use was identified in
> April, the database was conceptualized and designed in May, and the
> user interface was designed in June to July. The development of the system ran
> through June to August, after which system testing, the implementation of
> changes, and beta testing were carried out in August. The feedback gathered
> from the beta testers was implemented in September, and consultation with the
> adviser together with the finishing of the documentation were completed in
> September, marking the end of the planned development activities.
