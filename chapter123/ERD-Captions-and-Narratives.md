# 3.7 ENTITY RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM — Captions and Narratives

Captions and narrative paragraphs for the entity relationship diagram of
the proposed system, generated by `generate_erd_drawio.py`.
Captions sit **above** the figure (matching your reference document);
the narrative goes on the page immediately after. Because the full schema
has twenty-four entities, the diagram is split into four self-contained
figures so that every figure stays readable on a single page. The
`profiles` entity is repeated in each figure because it is the central
entity of the schema. Figure numbering continues from your Activity
Diagram figures (Figures 15–21); the Use Case Diagram in Section 3.8
continues at Figure 26.

---

## Figure 22

**Caption:** Figure 22: Entity Relationship Diagram (Accounts, Membership and Attendance)

**Narrative:**

> Figure 22 presents the accounts, membership and attendance part of the
> entity relationship diagram of the proposed system. The profiles entity
> is the central entity because it stores the account records of the
> Admin, the Trainer, and the Member. The enrollments entity stores the
> pending registration details and refers to profiles through the
> confirmed_by foreign key, which is optional because an enrollment is
> confirmed only after the Admin approves it. The memberships entity
> stores the plan name, the price, and the validity period of each
> membership. The trainer_assignments entity links each member to an
> assigned trainer through both the trainer_id and the member_id foreign
> keys, while the attendance and check_ins entities store the QR code
> check-in and check-out records, and the addresses entity stores the
> home address of each member.

## Figure 23

**Caption:** Figure 23: Entity Relationship Diagram (Fitness Tracking, Goals and Trainer Feedback)

**Narrative:**

> Figure 23 presents the fitness tracking, goals, and trainer feedback
> part of the entity relationship diagram. The workout_logs entity stores
> each exercise entry together with the sets, the repetitions, the
> weight, the duration, the proof video, and the computed calories; the
> body_measurements entity stores the weight, the height, and the body
> circumference readings; and the goals entity stores the fitness goals
> set by the member. The member_goal_plans entity stores the seven-day
> food plan and exercise plan assigned by the trainer, and the
> plan_day_completions entity records the per-day completion state of
> each plan, referring both to member_goal_plans through the plan_id
> foreign key and to profiles through the member_id foreign key. The
> trainer_feedback entity stores the written feedback of the trainer for
> each member; its trainer_id foreign key is optional because feedback is
> written only by trainer accounts.

## Figure 24

**Caption:** Figure 24: Entity Relationship Diagram (Nutrition, Meal Logging and Reference Data)

**Narrative:**

> Figure 24 presents the nutrition, meal logging, and reference data part
> of the entity relationship diagram. The meal_records and meal_logs
> entities store the logged meals and their nutrient values; the
> food_recommendations entity stores the generated food suggestions; and
> the food_identification_logs entity stores the AI photo recognition
> candidates and the member's selected correction. The met_exercises
> reference entity stores the exercise catalog together with the MET
> value of each exercise and the optional verifier through the
> verified_by foreign key. The nutrition_foods reference entity stores
> the verified Filipino dish composition data taken from the DOST-FNRI
> reference; it has no foreign key to profiles and is therefore shown as
> a standalone table in the figure.

## Figure 25

**Caption:** Figure 25: Entity Relationship Diagram (Communication, Notifications and Administration)

**Narrative:**

> Figure 25 presents the communication, notifications, and
> administration part of the entity relationship diagram. The chat_rooms
> entity stores each trainer-member conversation room through both the
> member_id and the trainer_id foreign keys, and the chat_messages entity
> stores the messages of each room, referring to chat_rooms through the
> room_id foreign key and to profiles through the sender_id foreign key.
> The notifications entity stores the system alerts sent to each account,
> the predictions entity stores the generated fitness progress
> predictions, and the admin_logs entity stores the activity log of the
> Admin through the admin_id foreign key.

---

Across Figures 22 to 25 the profiles entity is repeated as the central
entity because it stores the account records of the Admin, the Trainer,
and the Member, and almost every other entity refers to it through a
foreign key. The three optional relationships in the diagram are
enrollments to profiles through confirmed_by, trainer_feedback to
profiles through trainer_id, and met_exercises to profiles through
verified_by; all other relationships are mandatory one-to-many
relationships shown with crow's foot notation.
