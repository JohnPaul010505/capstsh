# 3.8 USE CASE — Captions and Narratives

Captions and narrative paragraphs for the use case of the proposed system,
generated by `generate_use_case_drawio.py`.
Captions sit **above** the figure (matching your reference document); the
narrative goes on the page immediately after. Following the three roles
defined in Chapter 1 (Admin, Member, and Trainer), the use case is
presented in three figures, one per role, in the same way the entity
relationship diagram of Section 3.7 is split. The Gemini API appears as a
secondary actor in Figure 26, and the AI-assisted use cases shared by more
than one role are drawn as «include» targets and repeated in each figure
where they are used, in the same way the profiles entity is repeated in
the entity relationship diagrams. The role-level use cases shared by more
than one figure, Login and Receive Notifications, are likewise repeated in
the figure of every role that can perform them.

## Figure 26

**Caption:** Figure 26: Use Case of the Member Module

**Narrative:**

> Figure 26 represents the access rights of the Member of the proposed
> system of Triple J Fitness Center. The Member can register and log in to
> the system, log workouts with an optional exercise proof video, perform
> the QR code check-in and check-out, view the member dashboard, view the
> BMI and body measurement records, set fitness goals, view the assigned
> goal plan and its per-day completion, view the generated progress
> prediction, chat with the assigned trainer, view the feedback of the
> trainer and rate it, receive notifications, and manage the profile and
> the settings.
>
> The dashed dependencies show the AI-assisted steps behind these use
> cases. The Log Meal use case includes Identify Food Photo, which is
> performed with the Gemini API as the secondary actor, together with
> Generate Food Recommendation and Compute Nutrient Breakdown; the Log
> Workout use case includes Estimate MET Energy Expenditure, which
> computes the calories burned; the Set Fitness Goal use case includes
> Generate Goal Suggestion; and the View Progress Prediction use case
> includes Generate Progress Prediction. The Record Exercise Proof Video
> use case extends the Log Workout use case because recording a proof
> video is optional.

## Figure 27

**Caption:** Figure 27: Use Case of the Trainer Module

**Narrative:**

> Figure 27 represents the access rights of the Trainer of the proposed
> system. The Trainer can log in to the trainer application, view the
> trainer dashboard together with the retention risk of the assigned
> members, view the assigned members and their member logs, record body
> measurements, create the seven-day food and exercise plan, monitor the
> plan completion, give written feedback to the members, chat with the
> members, receive notifications, and manage the trainer profile.
>
> The View Trainer Dashboard with Retention Risk use case includes Assess
> Retention Risk, and the Create 7-Day Food and Exercise Plan use case
> includes Generate Goal Suggestion and Search MET Exercise Catalog.
> These included use cases are performed by the AI service of the system,
> which supports the trainer in preparing the personalized plan of each
> member.

## Figure 28

**Caption:** Figure 28: Use Case of the Admin Module

**Narrative:**

> Figure 28 represents the access rights of the Admin of the proposed
> system. The Admin can log in to the administrative dashboard, manage
> the member records and the trainer records, assign a trainer to a
> member, confirm the pending enrollments, manage the membership records,
> view the attendance records and the workout records, generate the
> inactive member report and the coach feedback report, manage the QR
> codes used for the check-in, send and broadcast notifications, view the
> generated progress predictions, view the activity logs, and configure
> the system settings.
>
> The Generate Inactive Member Report use case includes Detect Inactive
> Members, and the View Progress Predictions use case includes Generate
> Progress Prediction, so the Admin always works with the computed result
> of the predictive analytics service instead of preparing the reports
> manually.

---

Across Figures 26 to 28 the solid lines represent the associations between
each actor and its use cases, while the dashed arrows represent the
«include» and «extend» dependencies of the AI-assisted steps. The shared
AI use cases, such as Generate Progress Prediction and Generate Goal
Suggestion, are repeated in the figures where they are used, in the same
way the profiles entity is repeated in the entity relationship diagrams of
Section 3.7. In the same manner, the Login and Receive Notifications use
cases are repeated in the figure of every role that can perform them.
